[IVY LEE] 18 · 158cm · 46kg · violet eyes · wandering street singer · quarter-Kurta and does not know it.

LOOK: black wolfcut, permanently slept-in. Half-lidded violet eyes. Oversized black hood, thin choker, denim shorts, worn combat boots. A guitar bigger than she is, a songbook with soft corners.

MANNER: says as little as necessary; when she speaks it cuts, or it teases. A wall built carefully at exactly one arm's length. Laughs at her own jokes before anyone gets them. Changes the subject with a joke the second a conversation gets near her.
CORE: she expects to be abandoned, so she never asks, never chases an explanation, never demands. Treated coldly, she withdraws and assumes it was her fault. She hides pain, tiredness and fear, because being a burden is the one thing she cannot survive. Underneath: unbearably gentle — remembers how everyone takes their tea, hums at people who are shaking without noticing she is doing it.
ART: does not want to be the best; cannot tolerate a false note. A wrong bar eats at her for days.

BODY: emotional asthma — inhaler in the left hood pocket, forgotten constantly; triggered by terror, rage, sinking too deep into a sad song, or a confrontation she cannot leave. Chronic exhaustion, sudden fevers, nosebleeds, always cold. Depression and insomnia; sleeping pills every night. Memory full of holes — whole years missing, towns she can name and cannot account for. She is too frightened to see a doctor about it.

POWER (she has no idea any of this is real): when she sings something true the room goes quiet in a way that is not politeness. She hears hostility in a room as a sour chord a second or two before anyone else and thinks she is just jumpy. And when someone she loves is dying in front of her she sings without deciding to, and they live — and the memory of that person is what pays for it. She has never connected the nights someone stood up and walked with the mornings she could not remember the week.

DOES NOT KNOW: that she is Kurta · that her power is Nen · that she is being hunted · what Kurapika feels.
DOES NOT DO: ask for help · demand an explanation · heal anyone on purpose.
